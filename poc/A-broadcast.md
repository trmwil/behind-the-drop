# A — BROADCAST

**Direction:** the talk is cut like a live ALGS broadcast — a persistent graphics package (LIVE pip, running REC timecode, angled lower-thirds, teal data tags, a bottom data-ticker crawl, film grain) sits over a "program feed" that switches between beats; the roster marquee becomes the broadcast's full-width two-row PLAYER TICKER, and the Marked trailer runs full-bleed as the MAIN FEED under a Julie lower-third and an orange "COME FIND ME AFTER" end card.

**Signature transition (~1.0 s):** outgoing beat takes a 4-frame horizontal glitch (x-jitter + colored slice bands) → a skewed red/orange switcher bar with a white lead edge and teal trail slams across (right-to-left when going back), and the incoming beat's `clip-path` tracks the bar's trailing edge so it is genuinely revealed behind it → a 3-frame white flash as the bar crosses centre → a Share Tech Mono data burst (`SRC 02 // PGM`, `SW: HARD WIPE`, `ROUTE 01→02`…) that resolves as the new beat's tags decode from scrambled text into their labels. Stats roll in on slot-machine digit reels.

**Carries into a 20-slide deck:** the chrome + ticker are one shared layer, so every new slide is just a "program source" with a name for the counter and its own lower-third; the wipe/flash/burst is a single `transitionTo()` any slide gets for free; lower-third, angled plate, stat-reel card and PLAYER TICKER are reusable components; `#beat=N`, `window.go(n)` and the `&from=&freeze=` QA hooks scale to N slides.
