# C — DROP SEQUENCE (in-world Apex UI)

**Direction in one sentence:** the talk is staged inside the game's own interface language — jumpmaster lobby → intel brief → legend select → the drop — so the room feels like it is sitting in a match lobby, built from original CSS/SVG (chamfered red-stripe panels, HUD brackets, compass strip, kill-feed ticker, ping markers, Share Tech Mono readouts) over real Steam/EA art only.

**Signature transitions (~1.0–1.1 s each, keyed per destination beat):** the **RING** — a hand-driven `clip-path: circle()` that closes over the outgoing beat and re-opens on the incoming one, with a scaled orange ring-edge glow, an outside-the-ring haze and a "RING CLOSING // STAND BY" callout; the **DROP** — the fully composed next beat falls in from scale 1.15 behind a vertical motion-line whip, then a two-frame impact shake; the **CHAMPION banner** — a wide skewed orange slab slams in from the right, holds ~300 ms over a dark flash while the beats swap, and wipes off left. On arrival the HUD brackets snap onto the new headline (`back.out`) and the compass heading whips to the beat's bearing.

**Roster reuse:** beat 3 *is* the legend-select carousel — the two-row seamless marquee (25 portraits, duplicated set + `translate3d(-50%)`), with a bracketed, scaled "SELECTED" tile chosen every 1.8 s from whichever tile is nearest row-centre, mirrored by a readout panel that crossfades the portrait and snaps the name/class in. Headline: "PICK YOUR SQUAD // it takes a roster to ship a season."

**Carries into a 20-slide deck:** the panel/bracket/feed grammar scales to any content slide; the three transitions map to slide "types" (ring = new chapter, drop = same chapter next point, banner = a reveal/punchline); the compass heading and beat counter become the running chrome; the roster carousel can be re-pointed at teams, platforms or seasons; the kill-feed is a ready-made stat/timeline component.

**Known limits / next:** headless Chrome throttles rAF under virtual time, so motion was verified through the built-in `?go=N&seek=MS` hook (frames in `_qa/C_dbg_*.png`) — real-browser playback still needs an eyes-on pass, especially the clip-path ring on an older projector GPU; the 720p Marked trailer is cropped 12% to hide its baked letterbox/disclaimer and softens slightly at 1920; countdown readouts (T-03 → GO) are placeholders for Julie's pacing; the "CHAMPION" banner copy and beat-3 squad-slot labels need her real narrative before they mean anything.
